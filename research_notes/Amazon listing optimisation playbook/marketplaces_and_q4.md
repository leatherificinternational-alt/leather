# Marketplace-specific rules (Amazon.ca, Amazon.com.au) and the Q4 2026 seller calendar (US/CA/AU)

Research date: 2026-10-17. Labels used: **[AMAZON]** = Amazon-owned page (sell.amazon.*, sellercentral help, Seller Central news, or reported quotes from them); **[GOV]** = government/industry association; **[FORUM]** = Seller Forums post (anecdotal); **[OPINION]** = agency or trade-press blog. Tooling limits: Seller Central help pages need a login and could not be read directly. Firecrawl was heavily rate-limited, so only 2 pages were scraped in full: the EcomCrew Q4 article and sell.amazon.com.au/brand-registry. Everything else comes from search-result snippets.

## 1. Amazon Canada: French/bilingual content, backend limits, listing sync, programs, pricing

### Takeaway
I found no Amazon.ca rule that requires French listing content for general merchandise such as leather goods. Amazon.ca does show French product pages, but they are often machine-translated or written by Amazon, and sellers report they cannot reliably edit them. The legal pressure comes from Quebec's Charter of the French Language (Bill 96 / Law 14). Industry sources say enforcement against online sellers with no establishment in Quebec is complaint-driven and incentive-based. Physical labels and enclosed documents are the bigger compliance exposure than listing text. Build International Listings (BIL) syncs offers and prices from US to CA by exchange rate. I found no source on how BIL treats overrides of titles or other attributes.

### Cited Findings
- **Bill 96 scope (legal).** Quebec's Charter of the French Language, strengthened by Bill 96 (now Law 14), expects commercial information offered to Quebec consumers to be in French, whoever is selling. Product inscriptions, packaging and enclosed documents (instructions, warranty) must be in French, and no other language may be more prominent than French. [OPINION] — [Accrue Agency](https://theaccrueagency.com/blog/amazon-ca-quebec-strategy-bill-96-and-french-listings/); [UNLS](https://unls.ca/blog/bill-96-business-french-requirements/)
- **Enforcement on out-of-province sellers.** The CFIB describes the OQLF's approach to e-commerce businesses without a Quebec location as incentive-based rather than mandatory, with complaint-driven enforcement. It also says products warehoused elsewhere in Canada but sold online into Quebec must meet French requirements. [GOV/industry association, read via secondary snippet] — [CFIB: Law 14 / Bill 96](https://www.cfib-fcei.ca/en/site/qc-law-14-bill-96); [Eski Media](https://eskimedia.com/bill-96-website-outside-quebec/)
- **Federal labelling (separate from Bill 96).** The Consumer Packaging and Labelling Act requires key information on many prepackaged consumer products sold anywhere in Canada, such as product identity and net quantity, to appear in English and French. Some exemptions apply. [OPINION summarising federal law] — [Weglot](https://www.weglot.com/blog/bill-96-explained)
- **Seller-forum view.** One seller thread worried that most products could not be sold into Quebec after June 1, 2025. A reply said Bill 96 language rules bind businesses located in Quebec and do not restrict direct-to-consumer internet sales from outside Quebec. This is not an Amazon position. [FORUM] — [Seller Forums CA: Quebec Language Laws – Bill 96](https://sellercentral.amazon.ca/seller-forums/discussions/t/b1c99d14-801b-4303-a76f-0e10620086c6)
- **Agency claim, no Amazon citation.** "Amazon requires a single bilingual listing", and creating separate ASINs per language "can violate policy". The agency calls bilingual listings "non-negotiable". [OPINION] — [My Amazon Guy](https://myamazonguy.com/amazon-canada/bilingual-product-listings-on-amazon-canada/)
- **How French pages behave in practice.** Sellers report that French pages are auto-translated when a shopper switches to French, but sometimes do not appear at all. Auto-translations can be inaccurate. An internal Amazon team writes some French bullets, which sellers cannot edit, and French-only feed uploads have failed. One post says food and certain other categories must be bilingual. [FORUM] — [thread a44c9d07](https://sellercentral.amazon.ca/seller-forums/discussions/t/a44c9d07-d408-49e0-bedf-72f8f1111039); [thread bbf5bb7e](https://sellercentral.amazon.ca/seller-forums/discussions/t/bbf5bb7e-26b6-4099-93b1-1b384b61300a); [thread 7fee2f0c](https://sellercentral.amazon.ca/seller-forums/discussions/t/7fee2f0c-26d4-4e0e-973a-9fc0b5687dbf); [thread 89766e33](https://sellercentral.amazon.ca/seller-forums/discussions/t/89766e33-a156-4d96-b3cf-cced6c8979cf)
- **Backend search terms.** Sources say 250 bytes per ASIN "in most marketplaces", which is commonly cited for the US. Accented characters (é, è, ç, à) take 2 or more bytes each in UTF-8, so French terms use up the limit faster. If the limit is exceeded, Amazon may ignore all backend terms, not just the excess. [OPINION] — [SellerSprite](https://www.sellersprite.com/en/blog/amazon-backend-search-terms); [Amalyze](https://amalyze.com/resources/guides/listing/amazon-listing-backend-search-terms); [My Amazon Guy](https://myamazonguy.com/seo/amazon-backend-keywords/)
- **Build International Listings.** BIL replicates offers from a source marketplace (for example US) to target marketplaces (for example CA), through Sell Globally > Build International Listings. [OPINION] — [Ecomclips](https://ecomclips.com/blog/diversify-from-amazon-us-to-amazon-ca-amazon-guide/). Amazon says BIL can translate listings into local languages for eligible offers. [AMAZON] — [Sell on Amazon: 7 steps for selling in Canada](https://sell.amazon.com/blog/how-to-sell-on-amazon-canada)
- **BIL pricing.** Target prices use same-day exchange rates and pricing rules. They update when the source price changes by more than 1%. BIL rules can be viewed and edited in Automate Pricing, and SKU-level rules are on Manage Pricing. [OPINION/trade press] — [ChannelX (2021)](https://channelx.world/2021/02/amazon-build-international-listings-pricing-rules-now-in-automate-pricing/); [ChannelX](https://channelx.world/?p=34615); [BQool](https://blog-cn.bqool.com/build-international-listings/)
- **BIL failure modes.** An ASIN with no active source offer, or one that fails auto-translation for Amazon.ca, does not sync. A pricing-rule error has blocked Remote Fulfillment enrolment for CA and MX. [FORUM] — [Seller Forums CA](https://sellercentral.amazon.ca/seller-forums/discussions/t/38656f44-cb60-4b87-ba10-d7122a6af1fe); [Seller Forums EU](https://sellercentral-europe.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/c485e2d4-f1fa-47b6-affc-958dbd17102d)
- **A+ and Brand Story on Amazon.ca.** Amazon.ca lists Brand Story as an A+ Content type. A+ Content requires a Professional account and a Brand Representative or Reseller role on a brand enrolled in Brand Registry. [AMAZON] — [sell.amazon.ca: A+ Content](https://sell.amazon.ca/tools/a-content)
- **Rufus in Canada.** Amazon launched a Rufus beta for a subset of Canadian app customers in late October 2024, alongside France, Germany, Italy and Spain, with wider rollout planned for the following weeks. [Trade press] — [TechCrunch, 2024-10-29](https://techcrunch.com/2024/10/29/amazon-brings-its-rufus-ai-shopping-assistant-to-more-international-markets); [Retail Insider](https://retail-insider.com/retail-insider/2024/10/amazon-launches-rufus-ai-shopping-assistant-in-canada/); [Canadian Grocer](https://canadiangrocer.com/amazon-launches-ai-powered-shopping-assistant-canada)

### Inferences
- For a leather-goods brand, the safest approach costs little. Put bilingual (EN/FR) information on the packaging, care cards and warranty, because those physical items carry the clearest Quebec exposure. Then make sure the French version of the Amazon.ca page is accurate, either through a French feed or flat file (language_tag fr_CA) or by checking the auto-translation, since a bad French page hurts conversion with Quebec shoppers. I did not verify the fr_CA tag mechanics.
- Backend terms on Amazon.ca: assume a 250-byte limit and count bytes, not characters, when adding French terms. A combined EN+FR set fills the limit quickly, so put French synonyms in French-language fields where possible.
- Listing sync: treat BIL as an offer and price sync. Write CA titles and bullets deliberately rather than relying on auto-translation. Because BIL tracks the US price by exchange rate, a US deal price can carry over into CA unless rules are set to stop it.
- Rufus has been live in some form in Canada since late 2024. So the same conversational-content practices used in the US (Q&A-style bullets, use-case language) carry over to CA. I could not confirm its status in 2026.

### Gaps
- Official Amazon.ca help pages on French content, the fr_CA language tag, and title/attribute override rules under BIL (login required, not retrievable).
- Whether the Amazon.ca backend limit is officially 250 bytes, and whether French terms belong in a separate fr_CA generic_keyword field.
- Vine availability on Amazon.ca, and whether Alexa+ shopping or a newer Rufus is live in Canada in 2026: no source found.
- Amazon.ca pricing-policy specifics. I found no CA-specific source on "lower price elsewhere" suppression or price parity across amazon.com and amazon.ca. Amazon's Fair Pricing Policy is understood to apply in every marketplace, but this was not verified for CA.
- The OQLF primary text was not scraped because of rate limits. Verify directly with the [Office québécois de la langue française](https://www.oqlf.gouv.qc.ca).

## 2. Amazon Australia: Brand Registry, brand tools, GST, assistant, limits

### Takeaway
A US brand does not need an Australian trademark to join Brand Registry. Amazon accepts a pending or registered mark from the trademark office of any country that has an Amazon store, which includes the USPTO, and one global Brand Registry account covers all marketplaces. Amazon does recommend adding a trademark for each country store you sell in. Once enrolled, A+ Content, Brand Stores, Sponsored Brands, Brand Analytics and Customer Reviews tools are available on Amazon.com.au. Prices for GST-taxable goods should be shown GST-inclusive.

### Cited Findings
- **Eligibility.** You need a brand name or logo permanently affixed to the product or packaging, plus "a pending or registered trademark for your brand name or logo that's issued by the designated government trademark office of a country with a corresponding Amazon store." [AMAZON] — [sell.amazon.com.au: Brand Registry](https://sell.amazon.com.au/brand-registry)
- **FAQ wording.** You need "an active, registered trademark or a pending trademark application", and "In some cases, you may also enrol with a pending trademark registration". The mark must be a word mark or a design mark with words, letters or numbers. Text must match the brand name exactly, and you must be the trademark owner (agents are added as users). [AMAZON] — [sell.amazon.com.au: Brand Registry FAQ](https://sell.amazon.com.au/brand-registry)
- **Multi-country accounts.** "Do I need to create multiple Brand Registry accounts if I sell in more than one country? No… single, global account… We recommend submitting trademarks for each country-based Amazon store you want to sell in." [AMAZON] — [sell.amazon.com.au: Brand Registry FAQ](https://sell.amazon.com.au/brand-registry)
- **IP Accelerator.** Amazon's IP Accelerator connects brands with Amazon-vetted law firms to file a trademark. It is listed on the AU site. [AMAZON] — [sell.amazon.com.au: IP Accelerator](https://sell.amazon.com.au/programs/ip-accelerator)
- **Brand tools available in AU.** A+ Content (video, enhanced images, comparison charts), Brand Stores, Sponsored Brands, Sponsored Display, Brand Analytics and Customer Reviews. Transparency and Project Zero are listed under protection. [AMAZON] — [sell.amazon.com.au: Brand Registry](https://sell.amazon.com.au/brand-registry)
- **New Seller Incentives (AU).** A 5% referral-fee credit on the first A$1.5M of eligible branded sales (worth up to A$75,000), plus a A$75 Sponsored Ads credit, for brands enrolled in Brand Registry that meet eligibility. [AMAZON] — [sell.amazon.com.au: Brand Registry / NSI](https://sell.amazon.com.au/brand-registry)
- **Conflicting reports on pending AU marks.** One AU seller was rejected twice while their IP Australia application was pending and got conflicting answers from support. Another reported approval only after the mark was granted. Some law-firm blogs say only registered marks qualify. [FORUM/OPINION] — [Seller Forums AU](https://sellercentral.amazon.com.au/seller-forums/discussions/t/424885ad4bf050b8a7bc7ce0e08197d3); [Seller Forums US](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/5ca59134-406a-458d-a570-498b7318b175); [Sprintlaw](https://sprintlaw.com.au/articles/amazon-brand-registry-in-australia-what-you-need-to-know/); [OpenLegal](https://openlegal.com.au/the-amazon-brand-registry-a-legal-guide/)
- **GST display.** Amazon.com.au has no "tax-inclusive" flag and assumes the listed price already includes any applicable GST. [OPINION – integrator guide] — [Maropost](https://galaxy.maropost.com/s/article/amazon-australia-and-gst-goods-and-services-tax). Under Australian consumer law, taxable consumer prices should be displayed GST-inclusive. [OPINION – law firm] — [Sprintlaw](https://sprintlaw.com.au/articles/gst-included-or-not-how-to-display-prices-in-australia/)
- **Low-value imported goods (LVIG).** Amazon calculates, collects and remits 10% GST on items of A$1,000 or less sold on Amazon.com and shipped from outside Australia. The GST is added to the price and shipping set in Seller Central. [AMAZON] — [sell.amazon.fr: Vente mondiale – Australie](https://sell.amazon.fr/vente-mondiale/australie)
- **GST registration.** The ATO threshold is A$75,000 annual GST turnover. [OPINION] — [EcomCalcTools](https://ecomcalctools.com/blog/fees-amazon/amazon-fees-australia-gst/)

### Inferences
- A US brand with a USPTO registration (or pending application) can enrol its AU presence under the existing global Brand Registry account and get A+, Brand Story, Store and Brand Analytics on Amazon.com.au. An IP Australia filing is still worth making for enforcement and Amazon's own recommendation, but it is not a prerequisite. Seller reports of rejections while an IP Australia mark was pending suggest using the USPTO mark if it is already registered.
- For FBA stock held in Australia, an Australian GST registration is likely needed once turnover passes A$75,000, with prices set GST-inclusive. The LVIG mechanism only covers items shipped from overseas.

### Gaps
- Rufus or any AI shopping assistant on Amazon.com.au: searches found no launch coverage for Australia. Treat as unconfirmed or not launched.
- Amazon Vine availability on Amazon.com.au was not confirmed.
- AU backend search-term byte limit, and title or other style differences from the US (for example Australian English spelling): no AU-specific source found.
- Amazon's country-specific Brand Registry requirements page (GHUDSVJLN4U9WY8Z) needs a login.

## 3. Global Selling: keeping content consistent, translation, separate vs linked offers

### Takeaway
Amazon's sync tool, BIL, syncs offers and prices and can auto-translate for eligible offers. Detail-page content is stored per marketplace, and translation quality is a recurring complaint from sellers. Brand Registry is one global account, but trademarks, A+ and Stores are managed per marketplace.

### Cited Findings
- BIL links offers across source and target marketplaces, with price rules keyed to FX rates and a re-sync when the source price moves more than 1%. [OPINION] — [ChannelX](https://channelx.world/2021/02/amazon-build-international-listings-pricing-rules-now-in-automate-pricing/); [BQool](https://blog-cn.bqool.com/build-international-listings/)
- Amazon says BIL can translate listings for eligible offers. [AMAZON] — [sell.amazon.com blog](https://sell.amazon.com/blog/how-to-sell-on-amazon-canada)
- Auto-translation on Amazon.ca is unreliable, and some French content is controlled by Amazon. [FORUM] — [Seller Forums CA](https://sellercentral.amazon.ca/seller-forums/discussions/t/bbf5bb7e-26b6-4099-93b1-1b384b61300a)
- A single global Brand Registry account is used, with trademarks added per country office. [AMAZON] — [sell.amazon.com.au](https://sell.amazon.com.au/brand-registry)

### Inferences
- Keep one master content source (for example a US flat file) and push CA and AU versions with deliberate edits for EN-CA/FR-CA and EN-AU spelling. Do not rely on BIL auto-population. Re-create or copy A+ content in each marketplace.

### Gaps
- Official Amazon documentation on BIL content sync, overrides and the paid "translation services" (Service Provider Network): none retrieved.

## 5. Listing-edit freeze during peak (reindexing risk)

### Takeaway
Amazon has no official rule freezing listing edits in Q4. The "freeze" is agency best practice: finish title, bullet, backend, variation and category changes before peak velocity (a commonly suggested freeze is around Oct 20), because large edits can trigger re-review or temporary indexing loss.

### Cited Findings
- Avoid significant listing changes once Q4 velocity is high, because large edits can temporarily suppress indexing and organic rank. Audit suppressed listings beforehand in the Listing Quality Dashboard. [OPINION] — [SellerLabs](https://www.sellerlabs.com/knowledge-base/seasonal-selling-on-amazon-planning-for-q4-and-beyond/)
- Complete titles first, then bullets and backend in one pass. Close variation merges and category changes early, refresh images and A+ by Oct 20, and publish a freeze date. [OPINION] — [My Amazon Guy](https://myamazonguy.com/amazon-account-management/amazon-q4-profit-margin-protection-checklist/)
- Avoid major listing changes (and banking-detail changes) in Q4. [OPINION] — [Riverbend Consulting](https://riverbendconsulting.com/blog/amazon-fba-q4-strategies/)

### Inferences
- For a US/CA/AU brand: lock US content by about Oct 20. For CA, edits made to the US source through BIL may propagate, so freeze both. AU's peak runs slightly earlier (Click Frenzy Nov 10 to 12, early Black Friday Nov 18), so freeze AU content by early November. Only fix suppressions and errors during peak.

### Gaps
- No Amazon-published statement or measured data on reindex times after edits.
