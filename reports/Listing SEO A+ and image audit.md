# Leatherific listing audit: keywords, Amazon AI readiness, A+ and images

*17 October 2026. Read-only check of the live pages on Amazon.com (all 7 product families), Amazon.ca (collar, mouse pad, coasters, leash, straps) and Amazon.com.au (collar).*

**How this was checked**

- **Keywords:** I matched your real ad search terms from 1 April to 16 October 2026 (SellerMate, 3,000+ terms) against each listing's text, word by word.
- **Images:** I read the text printed on 37 gallery images with an image reader. I looked at each main image, the A+ and the Brand Story in full-page screenshots.
- **Not checked:** backend search terms. They can only be read in Seller Central, and the Amazon Selling Partner connection is blocked in this session.

## 2. Questions Alexa for Shopping will ask

✓ = answered in the title or bullets. ~ = only in A+, description or attributes (weaker). ✗ = not answered anywhere.

- **Collar (US):**
  - **Missing:** how to measure (✗ in bullets; the A+ and size image cover it, inconsistently), smell (✗), wet or swimming use (✗), collar weight (✗).
  - **Conflicting:** the spike metal (brass vs alloy).
  - **A+ only:** suitable breeds and "not for small dogs".
- **Belt:** missing "runs small / size up?" ✗ (reviews say it does) and break-in or stiffness ✗.
- **Straps (US):** width, wrap type and uses appear only in the description (~). "How to use" is only on an image.
- **Mouse pad:** missing thickness ✗, care ✗ and smell ✗. The listing claims "no curling", but reviews say it curls and is "small and thin".

**Brand Story.** It's present on the collar, belt, straps and leash, but missing on the mouse pad, coasters, tray and everything in Canada and Australia. The hero background is a barbell and gym scene even on dog-collar pages. Make separate Brand Stories for the pet range and the gym range.

**Alt text.** On Amazon.com, seller-written A+ alt text still shows. Yours repeats keyword strings across images. Write one natural sentence per image, for example "Charcoal spiked leather dog collar on a Doberman, showing the brass buckle and ID ring".

## 6. Order of work

1. **Titles:** add "spiked" to every collar title (Magenta, Red, Green, CA, AU). That's your top search term, and it's free.
2. **Item Highlights and backend:** apply the titles and Item Highlights above, then paste the backend terms.
3. **Bullets:** cut to 5, no emojis, about 1,000 bytes, fix every contradiction in section 2, and add the missing answers (measuring, smell, runs small, break-in).
4. **Claims:** remove "USPA & IPL" (belt image 5 and A+ hero), "Waterproof" and "Electronic" (tray image), "Water/Heat Resistant" unless you can show proof, "eco-friendly", "top-rated", "Trusted worldwide".
5. **Main images:** fix the mouse pad, tray, coasters and belt. Collar optional.
6. **A+:** build the straps A+, coaster A+ and full mouse pad A+. Add FAQs to the collar and fix the leash comparison column. Rewrite the alt text.
7. **Leash:** add 6+ images and a video. Add videos for the belt, straps, mouse pad and tray.
8. **Brand Stories:** separate pet and gym Brand Stories, applied to every ASIN including the mouse pad, coasters and tray.
